Explain

2D potential-flow intuition, not CFD. Field = uniform flow + thin-airfoil bound circulation (5 vortices on the camber line, Γ = π·U·c·(α + 2h/c)) + thickness sources. Cl = 2π(α + 2h/c), faded smoothly past ~15° effective AoA (post-stall floor 0.3). Drag = profile + induced (AR 8, e 0.85) + separation term. Lift/drag magnitudes come from these formulas at chord 1 m, per metre of span. Particles colour by local speed or by Cp = 1 − (v/U)² (Bernoulli) — fast = low pressure. “Could you fly?” inverts the lift equation: S = m·g / (½ρv²·Cl), with the same Cl (and stall fade) as everything else.
