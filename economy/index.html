i got tired of numbers that all sound the same. "million", "billion" — same music, wildly different animals. so i drew them, at scale, along with french public spending and the fraud everyone loves to argue about.

chapter 2

02where french public money goes

france spent about €1,607bn of public money in 2023 (INSEE, comptes nationaux). here it is as 100 coins — each coin ≈ €16bn. hover a pile for what it actually covers.

the punchline: social protection is 53 of the 100 coins — more than half of everything. and it's not a mysterious black hole of "assistanat": it's mostly pensions (27 coins) and health (15 coins). your grandma and your hospital, basically. unemployment benefits — the thing the debate obsesses over — are about 3 coins.

sources & honesty notes

  • · INSEE, comptes nationaux — dépenses des administrations publiques 2023 (total €1,607bn) & PIB 2023 (~€2,822bn); déficit public 2024 ~€169bn (5.8% of GDP).
  • · DREES, comptes de la protection sociale 2023 — prestations ~€849bn, dont vieillesse-survie ~€427bn, santé ~€245bn.
  • · DGFiP, bilan fraude fiscale 2023 — €15.2bn mis en recouvrement (record).
  • · Cour des comptes / HCFiPS (2023–2024) — fraude sociale détectée ~€2.1bn (dont CAF ~€0.4bn); estimations fraude sociale ~€6–13bn; travail dissimulé (cotisations éludées) ~€6–8bn.
  • · Solidaires Finances Publiques — fraude et évasion fiscales estimées ~€80–120bn/an. contested: academic and Conseil des prélèvements obligatoires estimates are lower, but still several times any benefit-fraud figure.
  • · Vernimmen — dividendes CAC40 2023 ~€68bn (hors ~€30bn de rachats d'actions).
  • · AFT / INSEE — charge d'intérêts de la dette ~€51bn (2023, rising since).
  • · COR / gouvernement — réforme des retraites 2023: ~€18bn/an d'économies à l'horizon 2030 (projection).

honesty notes: "detected" fraud is what got caught, never the total; every "estimated" figure is shown as a range because the methods are genuinely contested; coins are rounded to the nearest €16bn so piles are ±1 coin; sector split mixes INSEE/COFOG and DREES groupings (health counted inside social protection, not twice). i'm a scientist, not an economist — check the sources, they're all public.
